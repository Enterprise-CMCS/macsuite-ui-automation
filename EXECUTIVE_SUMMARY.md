**Project:** Modernization of UI Test Automation Framework  
**Date:** April 8, 2026 (Week 2, Day 6)  
**Duration:** 6 weeks (30 business days)  
**Resource:** 1 QA Engineer (Full-time)

---

## Project Overview

We are modernizing our test automation framework to Playwright, Java, Cucumber, Allure reports and MS Copilot. This migration consolidates approximately 20 legacy test classes into 9 organized modules (~91 test scenarios) with faster execution, better reliability, reduced maintenance costs, and AI-assisted workflows that help accelerate test creation and make automation easier to maintain and expand.
